The Story

Built for farm decisions that cannot wait for perfect conditions

AgriDecision AI was created for producers who need business-grade answers from the numbers they already manage: acres, yields, prices, debt, costs, goals, and risk.

Why it exists

Farmers make high-stakes calls with narrow timing windows. A lender conversation, equipment purchase, hedge, land rent decision, or input plan can shift the whole year. AgriDecision AI turns scattered farm information into a clearer operating picture.

What it does

It combines saved project data, delayed commodity futures context, and configured AI providers to produce financial analysis, risk scenarios, investment thinking, and practical next steps for the operation.

01

Capture the operation

Save farm profile details, revenue, operating costs, debt, capital plans, and management goals under separate projects.

02

Analyze the numbers

Generate lender-style summaries with break-even thinking, cash-flow pressure, risk exposure, and investment recommendations.

03

Ask better questions

Use the AI tab to ask follow-up questions with the current project as context, including lender prep, expansion risk, and weekly priorities.

Built to travel with the user

With cloud accounts configured, AgriDecision AI saves profiles, projects, reports, and chat history behind sign-in so the same work can reload across devices. If cloud sync is not connected yet, the site keeps saving locally in the browser as a fallback.
